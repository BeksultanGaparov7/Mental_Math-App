# Dark worlds — detailed bible

Expands `WORLDS_DARK.md`. Everything here is original. Mechanics are identical in
all three worlds; only fiction, names and look change.

---

## 0. Shared structure (all three worlds)

### 0.1 What each rank unlocks

Ranks aren't just badges — each one opens a feature, so climbing changes how the game plays.

| Rank | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 1 | Training, daily review, placement tests, level exams |
| 2 | Bestiary + targeted hunts (mistake creatures appear from now on) |
| 3 | Skip exams (jump up to 2 levels) |
| 4 | The boss challenge (needs ≥ 3 creatures at tier 2+) |
| 5 | Companion's 2nd form · cosmetic shop · Vedic tricks drill |
| 6 | **Mixed Depths**: interleaved sessions across all drills, 1.5× XP |
| 7 | **Weekly Grand Boss**: built from your all-time worst patterns |
| 8 | **Sandbox**: build custom drills (choose operations, ranges, timer) |
| 9 | **Endless mode** (survive as long as you can, beat your own record) · companion's final form |

### 0.2 Story acts

The story is told in short scenes (3–5 lines plus one illustration) shown on rank-up.
Every world has three acts of three ranks each:

| Act | Ranks | Arc |
|---|---|---|
| I · Arrival | 1–3 | Thrown into danger, learn to survive |
| II · Standing | 4–6 | Earn a place, protect others, meet a rival force |
| III · Mastery | 7–9 | Face the source of the threat, change the world |

### 0.3 The eight core creatures

The mistake engine (`ADAPTIVE.md`) detects these patterns. Each world gives them a
body. **How it attacks** = which questions its Hunt and the boss serve you.

| Pattern tag | How it attacks |
|---|---|
| `carry_missed` | Sums whose columns carry, e.g. 47 + 38 |
| `borrow_missed` | Differences that borrow, especially across a zero |
| `fact:a×b` | That fact, its reverse, its division and ×10 forms |
| `table_neighbour` | Facts whose neighbouring answers are easy to confuse (7×8 vs 7×9) |
| `place_value_shift` | Products with zeros, decimals, % of round numbers |
| `digit_swap` | Answers with repeated or close digits (e.g. 1,263 vs 1,236) |
| `wrong_operation` | Mixed signs, rapid switching between + − × ÷ |
| `slow_fact` | Pure speed rounds on facts you know but hesitate on |

### 0.4 Daily loop (≈ 5 minutes)

1. Open the app → the companion speaks one line of story or advice.
2. **Daily review** (3 min, keeps the streak).
3. One suggested item: a ready exam, a Hunt, or the boss if available.
4. End screen: creatures that grew or shrank today, XP, progress to next rank.

## 1. The Hollow

### 1.1 World
- **The Summons.** Nobody knows who cast it. Since one night, ten years ago, it pulls a
  few thousand people a night into the Hollow, an endless drowned dream-city. Most
  return at dawn with nothing. Some return stronger. Some never wake.
- **The rule of the Hollow.** Everything there — walls, doors, monsters — is built from
  *unfinished equations*. To open a door you complete it; to wound a creature you name
  its true number faster than it can change.
- **The waking world.** The Summoned live normal lives by day. Ranks in the Hollow are
  respected in the waking world, because the Hollow is slowly leaking into it.

### 1.2 Places = drills

| Drill | Place | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Add · Subtract | **The Tidewalk**: flooded boulevards where you balance on numbered stones | water, balance |
| Multiply · Divide | **The Clockwork Nave**: a cathedral of turning gears and times-tables | rhythm, repetition |
| Squares · Roots | **The Stair of Mirrors**: every step reflects a number onto itself | symmetry |
| Fractions · % | **The Shattered Gallery**: broken paintings you reassemble into parts of a whole | pieces |
| Primes · Factors | **The Locked Wards**: doors that open only to indivisible keys | locks |
| Vedic tricks | **The Hermit's Lantern**: a recluse teaches forbidden shortcuts | secrets |

Levels are **Depths I–V**. Each Depth is sealed; deeper = harder, and darker visually.

### 1.3 Creatures (fractures)

Tiers: **Flicker** (1–4 misses) → **Hollowling** (5+) → **Fiend** (10+ or > 30 % miss rate)
→ **Abomination** (grew after a lost boss). A banished creature that returns within 14
days comes back one tier higher as a **Remnant**.

| Pattern | Creature | Look |
|---|---|---|
| carry missed | **The Carry-Eater** | a long eel that swallows the tens digit |
| borrow missed | **The Zero-Hollow** | a gaping mouth shaped like 0 |
| fact a×b | **Fact-Shades** (e.g. *Seven-Eight Shade*) | twin silhouettes fused at the shoulder |
| neighbour table | **The Mimic** | wears the face of the answer next door |
| place value | **The Ten-Shifter** | a crab that walks digits sideways |
| digit swap | **The Twins of Glass** | two mirrored children who swap places |
| wrong operation | **The Signbreaker** | a knight whose crest flips between + and × |
| slow fact | **The Drag** | heavy chains that slow your hands |

### 1.4 Challenges in story terms
- **Night Vigil** (daily review): the Summons always comes. Skip a night and your
  **Thread**, the line tying you to the waking world, frays (the streak breaks). An
  **Anchor** lets you sleep one night dreamless (streak freeze; earn 1 per 7 days).
- **First Descent** (placement): the Hollow tests how deep you can safely go.
- **Seal** (level exam) and **Breach** (skip exam).
- **Mending** (Hunt): close one fracture before it hatches further.
- **Nightmare Descent** (boss): your three worst creatures merge into one being. Its
  three heads attack in turn, and you have 3 **Breaths** (lives).
- **Ascension Rite** (rank-up): you stand at the Hollow's edge and the Summons weighs you.

### 1.5 Ranks and story beats

| # | Rank | Story beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Summoned | First night: you wake on a drowned street; a wisp leads you to the Refuge |
| 2 | Dreamer | You survive a week and learn that mistakes follow you home |
| 3 | Lucid | You see the equations inside the walls; Act I ends as you break your first deep Seal |
| 4 | Ashen | A night of fire in the Nave; you meet the **Choir**, other Summoned who hunt together |
| 5 | Hollowborn | The Hollow stops feeling hostile; the wisp grows a body |
| 6 | Warden | You guard a Refuge for new Summoned; Act II ends when the Choir's leader vanishes |
| 7 | Lanternbearer | You carry light into the deepest Depths and find who cast the Summons |
| 8 | Sovereign | A domain of the Hollow obeys your arithmetic |
| 9 | Unbound | You choose to keep coming back by choice, not curse; Endless mode opens |

**Companion — the Wisp:** a candle-sized flame (1) → a flame with a face (3) → a robed
wisp-knight (5) → a lantern-headed guardian (7) → a winged pillar of light (9).

**Visual mood:** midnight blues, cyan light, magenta for creatures, water reflections.
Closest to the earlier D · Shadow Dojo design, so the least redesign work.

## 2. The Ninefold Spire

### 2.1 World
- **The Rising.** One dawn, a tower of black glass grew through every major city, the
  same tower everywhere. Its door accepts anyone, but floors open only to exact minds.
- **The core.** Climbers grow a **soul core** behind the sternum, a small sun that
  brightens with every precise calculation. It grants strength, sharper perception and
  longer life. It also rots.
- **Rot.** A wrong answer leaves a speck of black glass in the core. Repeated, it
  spreads and becomes a living thing. Rot never kills, but it dims you and makes floors
  you already cleared feel heavier (more review).
- **Society.** Climbing houses compete; the Spire's summit has never been reached.

### 2.2 Places = drills (the six Paths)

| Drill | Path | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Add · Subtract | **Path of Tides**: endless stairs, add or subtract a step at a time | stamina |
| Multiply · Divide | **Path of Forges**: hammer rhythms, every blow a product | rhythm |
| Squares · Roots | **Path of Mirrors**: square halls whose sides are the root | geometry |
| Fractions · % | **Path of Shards**: divide light between broken prisms | balance |
| Primes · Factors | **Path of Keys**: indivisible keys for sealed doors | locks |
| Vedic tricks | **Path of the Old Masters**: murals of forgotten shortcuts | secrets |

Levels are **Floors I–V** of each Path.

### 2.3 Rot creatures

Tiers: **Taint** → **Blight** → **Canker** → **Corruption**. Returned = **Relapse**.

| Pattern | Creature | Look |
|---|---|---|
| carry missed | **The Lost Ember** | a dead spark that drops the tens |
| borrow missed | **The Hollow Debt** | a beggar of glass who takes from the next column |
| fact a×b | **Rot-Knots** (e.g. *Seven-Eight Knot*) | twisted black wire around the core |
| neighbour table | **The False Twin** | a reflection one step off |
| place value | **The Swollen Zero** | a bloated bead that multiplies by ten |
| digit swap | **The Weaver** | a spider that crosses threads |
| wrong operation | **The Turncoat** | a soldier whose banner switches sides |
| slow fact | **The Heavy Breath** | frost that slows the core's pulse |

### 2.4 Challenges in story terms
- **Breathing** (daily review): the core is tended by daily meditation. Streak = **the
  Flame**; freeze = **Banked Flame**.
- **Appraisal** (placement): the Spire's door weighs your core.
- **Floor Trial** (level exam); **Leap of the Stair** (skip exam).
- **Purging** (Hunt): burn out one rot in the core.
- **Tribulation** (boss): the Spire gathers lightning and strikes your three weakest
  points. Survive 20 strikes with 3 **Hearts**. Winning cleanses all three rots at once.
- **Core Ascension** (rank-up): your core changes colour before witnesses.

### 2.5 Ranks and story beats

| # | Rank | Story beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kindled | Your core lights on the threshold; an egg of black glass appears beside you |
| 2 | Tempered | You discover the first rot in your core and learn it can be purged |
| 3 | Forged | You clear your first Leap; Act I ends as a climbing house notices you |
| 4 | Honed | You join **House Meridian**; the first Tribulation strikes |
| 5 | Radiant | Your core glows visibly; the egg hatches into a lynx of light |
| 6 | Exalted | You protect younger climbers; Act II ends as a rival house's leader is consumed by Corruption |
| 7 | Paragon | You reach floors no one living has seen and find the Spire is alive |
| 8 | Ascendant | The Spire speaks to you, in numbers |
| 9 | Apex | You stand on the summit; the Spire lets you choose what it becomes. Endless mode opens |

**Companion — the Glass Beast:** black-glass egg (1) → cracked egg with a glowing eye
(3) → lynx of light (5) → winged lynx (7) → sky-serpent of prismatic glass (9).

**Visual mood:** black glass, gold light, ember orange; rot as sickly green-black veins.
Warmer and more "cultivation epic" than the other two.

## 3. The Eclipse Bastion

### 3.1 World
- **The Swallowing.** Forty years ago the sun went out over one night. What dimmed it
  is called the Eclipse; nothing outside the walls of the last city has been seen since.
- **Wards.** The city survives inside **Wards**: towers of brass and crystal that turn
  calculation into light and barrier. They need living minds; machines freeze in the
  Eclipse's cold.
- **The Order of Reckoners** keeps the Wards running. Each Reckoner holds one section of
  wall. Every wrong figure cracks it, and **Umbrals** — shapes of living dark — slip
  through and learn exactly the error that let them in.
- **Politics.** The Council of Lord Reckoners rules; rumours say someone inside the city
  wants the Wards to fail.

### 3.2 Places = drills (the six Wards)

| Drill | Ward | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Add · Subtract | **The Sum Gate**: the main gate, counterweights of brass | weight |
| Multiply · Divide | **The Lattice Tower**: crystal grids, products as light beams | grid |
| Squares · Roots | **The Square Keep**: a perfect cube of stone | geometry |
| Fractions · % | **The Rationing Hall**: divides light between districts | fairness |
| Primes · Factors | **The Prime Vault**: locks only primes open | locks |
| Vedic tricks | **The Archive of Ashes**: half-burned books of old methods | secrets |

Levels are **Rings I–V**, from the outer wall inward. Inner Rings protect more people.

### 3.3 Umbrals

Tiers: **Seep** → **Stalker** → **Devourer** → **Eclipse-spawn**. Returned = **Old Wound**.

| Pattern | Umbral | Look |
|---|---|---|
| carry missed | **The Tithe-Taker** | a hooded thing that steals one from every column |
| borrow missed | **The Hollow Creditor** | a coat full of empty pockets |
| fact a×b | **Gate-Hounds** (e.g. *Hound of Seven-Eight*) | dogs with a number burned on the collar |
| neighbour table | **The Changeling** | wears a neighbour's face |
| place value | **The Long Shadow** | stretches everything ten times too far |
| digit swap | **The Jester** | juggles two digits and swaps them |
| wrong operation | **The Traitor** | wears Reckoner robes, signs flipped |
| slow fact | **The Cold** | creeping frost on your hands |

### 3.4 Challenges in story terms
- **Night Watch** (daily review). Streak = **Unbroken Watch**; freeze = **Relief** (another
  Reckoner takes your watch).
- **Muster** (placement): the Order assigns your post.
- **Ward-Raising** (level exam); **Forced March** (skip exam).
- **Patrol** (Hunt): track one Umbral through the streets.
- **Siege Night** (boss): three Umbrals assault the gate together. The gate has 3
  **Bars**; each wrong answer breaks one. Hold for 20 waves.
- **Oath** (rank-up): sworn before the Council, which grows more suspicious as you rise.

### 3.5 Ranks and story beats

| # | Rank | Story beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ashling | You're found outside the walls as a child; the Order takes you in |
| 2 | Lamplighter | You keep street lamps alive and see your first Umbral slip through a crack |
| 3 | Watchman | You hold a wall section alone; Act I ends with a crack no one can explain |
| 4 | Sentinel | Siege Nights begin; you find a Ward sabotaged from inside |
| 5 | Bastion | You hold a gate alone through a siege; the moth becomes a lantern-hound |
| 6 | Warden | You command a ward-tower; Act II ends when you uncover a Council member feeding the Eclipse |
| 7 | Castellan | You protect a whole district while the city doubts the Order |
| 8 | Lord Reckoner | A seat on the Council; you expose the traitor |
| 9 | Sunbearer | You pour the city's combined calculation into a single Ward and light a small sun. Endless mode opens |

**Companion — the Lumen Moth:** a moth of living light (1) → moth with lantern wings
(3) → lantern-hound (5) → armoured hound with a crown of light (7) → a small captive
sun that follows you (9).

**Visual mood:** soot, brass, candlelight gold; Umbrals in violet. The most
grounded and political of the three, with a mystery plot (a traitor) driving the story.

## 4. Comparison

| | The Hollow | The Ninefold Spire | The Eclipse Bastion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core fantasy | Survive nightmares | Grow stronger, climb | Defend people |
| Tone | Eerie, lonely, mysterious | Epic, ambitious | Gothic, duty, intrigue |
| Mistakes feel like | Monsters that hunt you | Corruption inside you | Enemies breaking in |
| Boss | Nightmare Descent | Tribulation | Siege Night |
| Story hook | Who cast the Summons? | What is the Spire? | Who is the traitor? |
| Palette | Midnight + cyan + magenta | Black glass + gold + ember | Soot + brass gold + violet |
| Redesign effort | Low (closest to D · Shadow Dojo) | Medium | Medium |

## 5. Option: one world, or all three as a trilogy

Pick one world. Or, since all three appeal, use all three as a trilogy, one world per act:

| Ranks | World | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | **The Hollow** | Pulled into danger: survival and discovery |
| 4–6 | **The Ninefold Spire** | A Hollow Depth opens onto the Spire: growth and power |
| 7–9 | **The Eclipse Bastion** | The summit looks down on a city losing its sun: defending others |

- **Pro:** each Act feels new, with a fresh look and setting every three ranks, which helps you stay motivated over months.
- **Con:** about 3× the art and copy, and names must stay consistent across worlds (the mechanics stay the same, only the names change).
- **Middle ground:** build the MVP in one world (The Hollow, which needs the least redesign) and add the other two later as Act II and Act III.
