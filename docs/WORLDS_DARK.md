# Worlds I–III — dark fantasy (in the spirit of Shadow Slave, fully original)

All three share the same mechanics (see `SPEC.md`, `ADAPTIVE.md`); only the fiction,
names and look change. Each world has **9 ranks** on the shared ladder below.

## Shared 9-rank ladder (requirements)

| Rank | Requirement (cumulative) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Start (after the first placement test) |
| 2 | 300 XP · placement done in 3 drills |
| 3 | 2 drills at level II |
| 4 | 4 drills at level II · rank exam |
| 5 | 3 drills at level III · rank exam |
| 6 | 5 drills at level III · first boss win · rank exam |
| 7 | 3 drills at level IV · 3 boss wins · rank exam |
| 8 | 6 drills at level IV · no creature above tier 2 · rank exam |
| 9 | All drills at level V · final rank exam (10 min, all skills) |

Rank exams: mixed, ~5 min, ≥ 90 %; fail → 24 h cooldown. You never lose a rank.

## World I — The Hollow

**Premise.** A curse called *the Summons* drags people, one night at a time, into the
Hollow — a dream-realm of drowned cities. Its monsters are made of broken equations:
you only wound one by naming its true value, fast. You are newly Summoned. Survive,
climb, and wake stronger every morning.

**Why math = power.** In the Hollow, every strike is a calculation; hesitation is an
opening and a wrong answer is a *fracture* in your mind. Fractures don't heal on their
own — they hatch into creatures that hunt you on later nights.

| 5 | Hollowborn | Feels at home in the dark | teal |

## World II — The Ninefold Spire

**Premise.** One dawn a tower of black glass rose through every city on earth. Those
who climb it gain a **soul core** that grows with every perfect calculation; the Spire's
floors open only to minds that are fast and exact. Its nine tiers are the nine ranks.
Climbers who stop are slowly eaten by the rot of their own errors.

**Why math = power.** The core is literally sharpened by precision. A wrong answer
leaves a speck of **rot** in it; repeated, the rot spreads and takes form. Speed matters
because the Spire's trials are timed by its heartbeat.

## World III — The Eclipse Bastion

**Premise.** The sun was swallowed. Light now exists only inside the last walled city,
held up by **Wards** — great geometric engines that run on human calculation. You join
the **Order of Reckoners**, who keep the Wards alive through the endless night. Every
error cracks a Ward, and things from the dark slip through.

**Why math = power.** Wards are literally made of arithmetic; a Reckoner's speed is
the city's wall. Mistakes are cracks; ignored cracks let **Umbrals** in, and each Umbral
feeds on the exact kind of error that let it through.

## Why these stick (all three)
- **One story explains every rule**: cooldowns, creatures and bosses all have a reason, so nothing feels arbitrary.
- **Mistakes become enemies you can see**, so going back to fix weak spots feels like a hunt rather than homework.
- **Visible growth**: 9 ranks, an evolving companion, and creatures that shrink as you improve.
